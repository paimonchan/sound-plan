## Overview

GPT-SoVITS is **the most popular open-source AI voice tool on GitHub** — 56K stars, 90 contributors. Combines TTS + singing voice conversion. Few-shot training with 1 minute of audio. MIT license.

## Features

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Zero-shot TTS | 5 detik voice sample → langsung clone |
| Few-shot training | 1 menit audio → voice model kustom |
| Singing voice conversion | ✅ Built-in |
| WebUI | ✅ Gradio-based, included |
| Multi-language | CN, EN, JP, KR |
| Cross-lingual | ✅ |
| V2Pro | Latest (Jun 2025), improved quality |

## Why It's #1 (56K Stars)

- **Mature**: Sudah ada sejak Jan 2024, lebih established dari model 2026 manapun
- **Mudah**: WebUI bawaannya siap pakai
- **Fleksibel**: Bisa TTS conversation + singing voice
- **Murah**: Few-shot cuma butuh 1 menit audio
- **MIT**: Fully open, komersial bebas
